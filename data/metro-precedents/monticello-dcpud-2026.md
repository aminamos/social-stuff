# Monticello DCPUD (Data Center Planned Unit Development) — 2026

Metro precedent tracked for rural counties fielding data center proposals. Wright County is metro (Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington, OMB 23-01), so it sits outside the planning-bot 60 nonmetro county D1 scope. Recorded here as a model ordinance plus a live application to watch.

## 1. Ordinance identity

* City of Monticello, Wright County, MN. Ordinance 865, amending Title XV, Chapter 153 (Zoning Ordinance), sections 153.012 (Definitions), 153.045 (Industrial Base Districts), 153.046 (Overlay Districts), 153.090 (Use Table), 153.091 (Use-Specific Standards), 153.092 (Accessory Use Standards).
* Creates the Data Center Planned Unit Development (DCPUD) zoning district.
* Adopted 4-1 on April 27, 2026 (reported April 28, 2026).
* Full text: City of Monticello Ordinance 865 Adopted (PDF, verified HTTP 200 on 2026-10-06).
* Application forms: DCPUD Rezoning Application Checklist, dated June 25, 2026 (PDF, verified HTTP 200 on 2026-10-06).

## 2. Site and live application (Monticello Tech)

* Applicant: Monticello Tech, LLC.
* Site: approximately 547 acres, south of 85th Street NE and east of Highway 25.
* Jurisdiction: currently in Monticello Township but inside the Monticello Orderly Annexation Area (MOAA), eligible for annexation into the city.
* Proposal: technology campus (data center campus), approximately 3 million square feet, up to 8 data center buildings in phases over several years. End user not publicly identified; building level details (architecture, electrical demand, employment, parking, backup power placement) deferred to later site plan review.
* Three applications moving together: (a) rezone to DCPUD, (b) DCPUD conditional use permit (CUP), (c) preliminary plat. Final plat is deferred to a future application and is not part of the current review.
* Completeness: first submission July 6, 2026 ruled incomplete July 20 (clarified July 23). Second submission August 3 plus August 11 supplement ruled complete as of August 11, confirmed by city letter August 18, 2026.
* Review clock: city extended to the full 120 days. Decision due by December 8, 2026.
* Public hearing: Planning Commission, Tuesday October 6, 2026 at 6 p.m., Mississippi Room, Monticello Community Center, 505 Walnut Street. Written comment to Community.Development@MonticelloMN.gov by 4 p.m. October 6. Planning Commission is advisory; City Council makes the final decision.
* Project page (verified HTTP 200 on 2026-10-06): City of Monticello, Monticello Tech (ID 753), with submissions 1 (incomplete) and 2 (complete) posted.

## 3. District mechanics

* Where allowed: land guided Light Industrial Park in the Monticello 2040 Vision + Plan and zoned I-1 (Light Industrial) at time of rezoning application. No inherent right to rezoning.
* Findings required for rezone (a through m): plan/zoning fit, city water/sewer capacity with no shortage to existing or planned areas including the MOAA, electric supply demonstrated with the local supplier by phase, vehicular and non-vehicular facilities extended to serve adjoining future development, no displacement of industrial land the city deems critical, identifiable public benefit (tax base) without long term negative impacts, exterior noise/lighting compliance, all other chapter sections met, state PCA/DNR/Health/DOT requirements met to the city’s satisfaction, no conflict with other Comprehensive Plan elements.
* CUP only after DCPUD zoning. No work permit until minimum district performance standards plus conditions are satisfied to the city’s satisfaction.
* Principal uses (by CUP): Data Center, Data Center Campus.
* Accessory uses: offices, security buildings, support facilities (generators, mechanical/electrical, substations, transmission structures), off street parking per 153.067 Schedule 2, signs/fencing/loading per chapter except as restricted here, roof solar or green roof, EV charging, private wireless antenna/support (setback/height per this section), other subordinate customarily incidental uses by CUP.
* Prohibited: cargo/shipping/rail/semi containers or buildings not meeting section building standards (temporary post frame construction buildings allowed as temporary uses), commercial wind, commercial telecom towers, ground mounted solar, outdoor storage as defined, data mining as defined.
* Temporary uses: construction staging and activity tied to the active phase, construction material storage and offices tied to the phase or to replacement. Hours 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. Monday through Saturday, lighting per chapter.
* Environmental review: required EAW/EIS/AUAR must be complete before application (project level); component level state triggered review may follow. Regulated by state law plus chapter provisions.
* Money: applicant/developer/owner/operator bears all development and infrastructure costs attributable to the project, with financial guarantees as a condition. Utilities extended to DCPUD limits (or city determined extension point) in phase 1 unless council expressly approves otherwise, so later phases or neighbors are not stranded. Council record states no city tax incentives for data centers.

## 4. Performance standards (minimums; variance under 153.028 to deviate)

Definitions: data center (principal facility plus accessory/appurtenant offices, air handlers, backup generators, water cooling/storage, substations, supporting infrastructure; excludes accessory computing and data mining); data center campus (multiple principal buildings interconnected by power/comms/generation/operations into one facility); DCFAR (gross floor area of principal plus accessory data center buildings divided by net land area, where net excludes stormwater ponding/public waters to OHWL, delineated wetlands, public improvement easements, platted ROW, and required setback/buffer land; substations, parking and similar non buildings excluded; nearest 0.1 acre); data mining (temporary/portable sub 20,000 sq ft crypto style facilities without permanent industrial construction or onsite employment).

Floor area minimums (per DCPUD and per developed phase):

| Site size | Min DCFAR |
|---|---|
| Under 100 acres | 0.25 |
| 100 to 199 acres | 0.24 |
| 200 to 299 acres | 0.23 |
| 300 to 399 acres | 0.22 |
| 400 to 499 acres | 0.21 |
| 500+ acres | 0.20 |

Setbacks (structure or use to DCPUD property line; greater setback applies where an intervening street/ROW separates parcels; agricultural parcels use the farm column):

| Structure or use | Ag only | Residential/civic/institutional/mixed | Public park/recreation | Commercial | Industrial |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Principal building, mechanical fully screened by building, solid wall from all boundary points, inside a principal building, or ground mounted on an interior facade | 100 ft | 200 ft (300 ft if site over 300 gross acres) | 200 ft | 100 ft | 100 ft |
| Principal building with exterior oriented unscreened mechanical (generators, cooling) | 200 ft | 500 ft (700 ft if site over 300 gross acres) | 500 ft | 200 ft | 100 ft |
| Accessory use equipment, same unscreened case | 200 ft | 500 ft (700 ft if site over 300 gross acres) | 500 ft | 200 ft | 100 ft |
| Off street parking incl. aisles | 100 ft | 150 ft | 150 ft | 50 ft | 50 ft |
| Off street loading | 100 ft | 150 ft | 150 ft | 100 ft | 100 ft |
| Other accessory buildings/structures (lighting excepted) | 50 ft | 100 ft | 100 ft | 50 ft | 50 ft |
| Fences/walls | Any location except drainage/utility easement without license or designated wetland buffer | Same | Same | Same | Same |

Height: principal and accessory structures 50 ft max. Accessory and temporary structures 25 ft max. Private telecom antenna to 100 ft max. Substation equipment exempt.

Exteriors: principal buildings in decorative rock face block, glass, cast in place or precast concrete panels, or brick. Accessory buildings add exterior insulated finish systems. Construction offices must meet accessory materials.

Noise: design and build with sound mitigation; compliance with Minnesota PCA standards and rules at the DCPUD boundary. Third party ambient study before approval; equipment calculation study at neighboring property lines before operation (mitigation plans if over limits); operator liaison for complaints after operation starts; annual third party peak operation study with results to the city within 30 days of request (or proof of contract plus delivery in reasonable time); low frequency Noise Impact Statement under City Code 130.09(D) if the city requires it.

Generators: backup supply during power outage only (curtailment or voluntary shutdown is not an outage). Testing 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday through Friday only, annual schedule filed by January 15. Onsite fuel storage meets accessory setback/height/screening plus MPCA, State Fire Marshal, and applicable codes such as NFPA 58.

Lighting: chapter 153.063 plus zero footcandles at property lines abutting residential, civic, institutional, recreational, or parkland uses; no exterior source within 50 ft of such lines; 153.063(G) security plan exemptions not available in DCPUD.

Screening: outdoor facilities and equipment (backup generators, parking/circulation, staging, temporary buildings, any mechanical regardless of location, security or chain link fence within 200 ft of the DCPUD line, similar) fully screened to 100 percent opacity at comparable grade from abutting residential or recreational property and from public ROW except designated entrances. Screening by fence/wall meeting section materials, landscaping, berming, or a combination. Screening landscaping by a qualified landscape architect, opacity within 3 years, countable toward minimum site landscaping. Chain link with slats is not screening.

Site landscaping: chapter minimums plus sod/seed on unused ground (approved natives excepted), irrigation on landscaped areas (approved natives excepted), no island delineation requirement, natives encouraged.

Perimeter buffer: full DCPUD perimeter for the life of the facility, installed with phase 1 before other construction (designated construction entrances excepted), required even across intervening roadways/ROW. Minimum 10 ft berm above adjacent roadway centerline or adjacent property line elevation (whichever higher), two staggered evergreen canopy rows at 6 ft on center on the exterior downslope of the berm, meeting chapter size rules. May sit inside the structure setback, outside any screening wall. Not required directly abutting existing industrial use; required against vacant land, ROW, and all other uses. Natives and species variation encouraged.

Fencing/walls: maintenance free vinyl, ornamental metal (wrought iron or comparable), brick, stone, masonry, or stamped/colored concrete mimicking those. Black or black vinyl coated chain link for security only, no slats.

Parking: employees/service personnel per 153.067 Schedule 2.

Substations serving the DCPUD: inside the DCPUD boundary, accessory setbacks, section lighting and perimeter buffer apply; transmission extensions to the substation exempt from 153.065 undergrounding; otherwise exempt from section minimums.

Process: optional concept submittal (contacts, site data, objectives/public value narrative, uses/systems narrative, infrastructure needs and timing, transmission routing if known, buildout FAR estimate, schedule/phasing, stakeholder engagement plan for sites over 20 acres, concept plan with boundary, lots/uses, wetlands/watercourses to 200 ft, streets, sidewalks/trails, substations, woods/significant features, utility systems and routing to the DCPUD limits, access points). Joint Planning Commission/City Council workshop with 350 ft mailed notice plus public comment (not a hearing, no decision). Neighborhood meeting encouraged before formal rezoning filing. Rezone noticed as a PUD under 153.027; CUP noticed as a CUP under 153.027; the two may run concurrently. Preliminary/final plats under Chapter 152. Adopted fees and escrows apply. Annexation time overlay requirements still apply.
