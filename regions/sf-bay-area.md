# San Francisco Bay Area

The San Francisco Bay Area features civic tech brigades tackling local services, disability access, seismic safety, and regional transit data modeling across the 9-county metropolitan area.

---

## 🟢 Highly Active Organizations with Open Issues

### 1. Code for San Francisco (`sfbrigade`)
* **Focus**: The official San Francisco civic tech brigade working directly on community and volunteer apps for SF residents.
* **Primary Repos**:
  * [`sfbrigade/resource-binder-app`](https://github.com/sfbrigade/resource-binder-app)
  * [`sfbrigade/datasci-earthquake`](https://github.com/sfbrigade/datasci-earthquake)

### 2. Bay Area Metro (`BayAreaMetro`)
* **Focus**: The Metropolitan Transportation Commission (MTC) and Association of Bay Area Governments (ABAG). Develops open-source regional travel demand models and travel diary survey tooling across 9 Bay Area counties.
* **Primary Repos**:
  * [`BayAreaMetro/travel-model-one`](https://github.com/BayAreaMetro/travel-model-one)
  * [`BayAreaMetro/travel-diary-survey-tools`](https://github.com/BayAreaMetro/travel-diary-survey-tools)
* **Stack**: Python, Java, R, Bash
* **Activity**: Highly active in August–September 2026.
* **Target Issues**:
  * **[#113](https://github.com/BayAreaMetro/travel-model-one/issues/113) — `Typo large-truck blended travel time calculation in TruckTripDistribution`**
    * *Why solve*: Clear bug / typo in calculation logic affecting regional truck routing models.
    * *Skills*: Python.
  * **[#99](https://github.com/BayAreaMetro/travel-diary-survey-tools/issues/99) — `Add num_joint_trips to tours so partly-shared tours are discoverable`**
    * *Skills*: Python, Pandas.
