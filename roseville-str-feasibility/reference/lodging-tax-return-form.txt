Taxes shall be paid to the City of Roseville no later than 25 days after the end of the month in which the taxes were 
collected. If any tax payment is not made within the time specified by Chapter 302 of the Roseville City Code, 
there shall be a specific penalty equal to 10% of the amount remaining unpaid. The amount of tax not timely paid, 
together with any penalty added, shall bear interest at the rate of 10% per annum from the time such tax should 
have been paid until it is paid. Any interest and penalty shall be added to the tax and be collected as part thereof.
